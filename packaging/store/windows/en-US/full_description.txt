Obxodka is a modern, ultra-fast, and secure next-generation VPN client built on C# (.NET), engineered to deliver online privacy, seamless network resilience, and low-latency access to global web resources.

Enjoy lightning-fast connection speeds, instant response times in online gaming, and a sleek dark theme perfectly tailored for Windows 10/11.

---

🔒 TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES & ARCHITECTURE:

🛡️ Mutual TLS (mTLS) & AES-256-GCM
Individual client X.509 certificates authenticate every transport-level connection. All payload packets are secured with AES-256-GCM encryption. Private keys remain protected in your OS certificate store.

🙈 OBXODKA-STREAM Protocol (Next-Gen Traffic Stealth)
Your network traffic avoids traditional VPN signatures. Encapsulated in optimized duplex HTTP/2 streams over TLS 1.3, it mimics standard browser web sessions. Micro-segmentation desynchronization (DpiBypassStream) and dynamic SNI pools keep the tunnel undetectable by deep packet inspection (DPI) filters.

🐙 Dual-Ray Hedging Architecture (Multi-Stream Packet Redundancy)
The Octopus engine parallelizes and hedges packets across independent streams (Rays), eliminating bufferbloat and gaming packet loss. If an ISP drops packets on one channel, duplicates arrive instantaneously via the secondary ray.

⚡ Smart Adaptive MTU Escalation
Automated in-tunnel MTU probing discovers the optimal maximum packet size for your network provider, avoiding carrier packet black holes and maximizing transfer speeds.
