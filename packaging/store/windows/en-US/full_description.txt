Obxodka is a modern, ultra-fast, and secure next-generation VPN client built on C# (.NET), engineered to deliver online privacy, seamless network resilience, and low-latency access to global web resources.

Enjoy lightning-fast connection speeds, instant response times in online gaming, and a sleek dark theme perfectly tailored for Windows 10/11.

---

🔒 TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES & ARCHITECTURE:

🛡️ Mutual TLS (mTLS) & AES-256-GCM
Individual client X.509 certificates authenticate every transport-level connection. All payload packets are secured with AES-256-GCM encryption. Private keys remain protected in your OS certificate store.

🙈 DPI & ISP Filtering Resilience (Advanced Traffic Stealth)
Your network traffic avoids traditional VPN signatures. Encapsulated in native gRPC over HTTP/2 (TCP) or HTTP/3 QUIC (UDP), it mimics standard browser HTTPS requests to cloud endpoints.

🐙 Octopus Multi-Ray Architecture (8 Concurrent Rays)
The high-performance Kestrel core parallelizes traffic across independent Rays, reducing bufferbloat and maximizing throughput up to 150+ Mbps.

⚡ HTTP/3 QUIC & HTTP/2 Support
Seamlessly switch protocols. QUIC eliminates Head-of-Line blocking, ensuring rock-solid stability even on jittery cellular connections.
