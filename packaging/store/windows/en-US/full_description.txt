Obxodka is a modern, high-performance VPN client built on C# (.NET), engineered to deliver privacy, connection resilience, and low-latency network performance.

Enjoy low connection latency, zero packet loss, and a clean Fluent Design interface tailored for Windows 10 and Windows 11.


TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS & ARCHITECTURE:

1. Mutual TLS (mTLS) & AES-256-GCM Encryption
Individual client X.509 certificates authenticate every transport-level connection (L4). All payload packets are secured with AES-256-GCM encryption. Private keys remain protected in your operating system certificate store.

2. OBXODKA-STREAM Protocol & DPI Bypass
Network traffic is encapsulated in optimized duplex HTTP/2 streams over TLS 1.3, masquerading as regular web browsing sessions. Micro-segmentation packet desynchronization (DpiBypassStream) and a dynamic trusted SNI pool ensure tunnel resilience against deep packet inspection (DPI) systems.

3. Dual-Ray Hedging Architecture (Stream Redundancy)
The Octopus networking engine parallelizes and hedges packets across independent streams (Rays), minimizing bufferbloat and gaming packet loss. If packets are dropped on one route, duplicate frames arrive instantaneously via the secondary ray.

4. Smart Adaptive MTU Calibration
Automated in-tunnel MTU probing continuously determines the optimal packet size for your network provider, preventing packet fragmentation black holes and maximizing throughput.
