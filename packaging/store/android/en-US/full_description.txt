Obxodka VPN is a modern, high-performance solution engineered to safeguard digital privacy and ensure continuous, unrestricted internet access.

Designed to prevent ISP throttling and connection drops, Obxodka delivers fast data transfer speeds and enterprise-grade encryption in a single tap.


KEY ADVANTAGES:

1. High Speed and Low Latency
An optimized network stack and direct routing ensure smooth high-resolution streaming, fast file downloads, and lag-free online mobile gaming.

2. Privacy and Data Protection
Strict No-Logs policy. All connection traffic is protected with modern cryptographic ciphers (AES-256-GCM / ChaCha20-Poly1305), keeping your sensitive information safe on public Wi-Fi networks.

3. Resistance to Deep Packet Inspection (DPI)
Proprietary traffic masking with a dynamic pool of trusted SNIs keeps the encrypted tunnel resilient against carrier-grade deep packet inspection filters.

4. Split Tunneling
Custom routing control: choose which applications route through the secure tunnel and which connect directly to your local carrier network.

5. Adaptive MTU Calibration
Intelligent MTU sizing automatically adapts to mobile cellular networks (LTE/5G/Wi-Fi), avoiding packet fragmentation and dropped packets during cell handovers.

6. One-Tap Simplicity
Intuitive and minimalist interface requiring zero manual configuration.
